ABOUT
This is the homepage of Isaac Meyer. I am a software engineer at Radiant Industries, a nuclear microreactor company, where I work on our set of low order simulation tools for multiphysics simulation (dubbed the "digital twin"). I completed a post-doc at Massachusetts General Hospital in the Radiation Oncology department focusing on Monte Carlo transport, and I obtained my Ph.D. at MIT in the department of Nuclear Science and Engineering where my focus was uncertainty propagation of neutron cross section data.
